These are the rules for using Stepwell, an independent project. By creating
an account, you agree to them.

## Who can use Stepwell
You need to be 13 or older and give your real age range when you sign up.

## Your account
- Keep your password to yourself. One person per account.
- You're responsible for what happens in your account. If you think someone
  else got in, reset your password.
- You can delete your account anytime from your account page.

## Using Stepwell fairly
Don't:
- try to get into other people's accounts or information;
- attack, overload or copy Stepwell with bots or scripts;
- pretend to be someone else.

We may close accounts that break these rules.

## About the information on Stepwell
Career, program and college information comes from official U.S. government
sources such as O\*NET (U.S. Department of Labor) and College Scorecard (U.S.
Department of Education). It can be out of date or incomplete, and it's a
starting point, not a promise about any job, school or cost. Scholarship
listings come from official state and federal program pages, and internship
postings from USAJOBS (U.S. Office of Personnel Management); both can change
at any time, so always check the official page before you apply. Check important
details with the school or program, and talk with a counselor or a trusted
adult.

## Changes and availability
Stepwell is free and offered as it is. We may change it, pause it or stop it.
If we change these terms, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
