Stepwell helps students connect careers to programs and colleges. It's free.
It's an independent project. Questions: [CONTACT EMAIL].

## What we collect
- **Your email address and password.** You need them to sign in. Your
  password is stored scrambled (hashed), so no one can read it, including us.
- **That you're 13 or older.** When you sign up, we ask for the month and year
  you were born. We use them once to check your age and don't save them. We
  only save that you're 13 or older.
- **Your profile, if you fill it in.** A nickname (optional), where you are in
  school, your state, and the year you expect to graduate (optional). We use
  it to suggest careers, programs and colleges that fit you.
- **Scholarship answers, only if you turn on matching.** If you choose to,
  you can tell us your GPA, fields you might study, whether you'd be the first
  in your family to go to college, your citizenship status, whether a parent
  served in the military, and whether you plan to file the FAFSA. Every
  question is optional. We use the answers only to show which scholarships
  you might qualify for. They're never shown to anyone, shared, or sent to
  other companies or to AI tools.
- **Your interest quiz scores, if you save them.** Just the six scores, never
  your answers.
- **Careers, scholarships and colleges you save, and your plan.** The steps
  you add to your plan (a short title, the school year, when in the year, the
  kind of step and an optional due date), and whether you've done them. Only
  you can see them.
- **Cookies Stepwell needs to work.** A login cookie keeps you signed in until
  you sign out. If our age check says you're under 13, a cookie that ends when
  you close your browser keeps showing that message. Neither one tracks you.

## A check that you're a person
When you sign up, sign in, reset your password or delete your account,
Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're a person and not a bot. For that check,
Cloudflare sees details such as your IP address and your browser. Stepwell
doesn't get that information.

## What we never do
- No ads.
- No tracking or analytics tools.
- We never sell your information or share it with colleges, companies or
  anyone else for their own use.

## Who helps us run Stepwell
These services handle your information only to run Stepwell for us:
- **Supabase** stores your account and profile.
- **Vercel** runs the website.
- **Cloudflare** runs the person check above.
- **[EMAIL PROVIDER]** sends our emails (only to confirm your email address or
  reset your password; never newsletters or ads).

Like most websites, these services keep short technical records (such as IP
addresses) to keep Stepwell working and safe.

## How long we keep it
Until you delete your account. You can do that anytime from your account page:
it removes your login, your profile, and everything you saved right away.
Scholarship answers are also deleted if you don't sign in for 12 months, and
you can remove them yourself anytime on the "Scholarship matching" page.

## Your choices
- See and change your profile on your account page.
- Change or remove your scholarship answers on the "Scholarship matching" page.
- Delete your account and everything in it on your account page.
- Questions or requests: [CONTACT EMAIL].

## Under 13
Stepwell is for people 13 and older. If we learn that someone under 13 created
an account, we delete it. Parents can contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].
